About Vaultly

Secure file and document management, built for people who take their data seriously.

Vaultly gives you a single, secure place to store, organize, and retrieve the documents that matter most — financial statements, contracts, receipts, and personal records — protected behind two-factor authentication and encrypted-at-rest storage practices.

We started Vaultly with a simple idea: managing your important files shouldn't require juggling a dozen different tools. Whether you're archiving a year of invoices or keeping tax documents organized for the next filing season, Vaultly keeps everything a click away.
